01 / HARDWARE2R WRIST MODULE

WHY DISTAL REDUNDANCY?

Put orientation
where it belongs.

A 7-DoF arm is redundant for a six-dimensional end-effector pose, but its redundancy is distributed across long links. Adding two compact rotational axes at the tool changes the geometry of the problem.

01

Reorient the probe without sweeping the elbow through the patient workspace.

02

Preserve the main-arm posture while maintaining contact at a fixed point.

03

Rotate the imaging plane 90° locally instead of reorganizing the entire arm.

02 / MOTION STUDY7-DoF VS 9-DoF

Why we need extra 2 DoF?

7-DoF baseline9-DoF + wrist15 N normal force

The position target and surface-normal force are shared. The difference is where the orientation motion goes. All torques are produced by Isaac Lab's official Operational Space Controller; the 9-DoF nullspace target biases the added wrist while keeping the seven main joints near their phase-start posture.
